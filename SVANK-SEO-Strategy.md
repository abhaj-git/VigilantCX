# SVANK SEO strategy

## Positioning

**Accessible high fashion:** distinctive, polished sunglasses at $34.99, with free US and Canada shipping when customers buy two.

Brand idea:

- **S** — Style
- **V** — Visions
- **A** — Artistry
- **N** — Nouveau
- **K** — Kraft

The broad audience is adults of all ages. Marketing and search content should focus primarily on fashion-conscious adults ages 18–44, without excluding older shoppers. A defined primary audience produces clearer content and stronger campaigns than “everyone.”

## Product taxonomy

Primary navigation and collections:

1. Shop all
2. Polarized
3. Rectangle & square
4. Round & oval
5. Cat-eye
6. Geometric & browline
7. Black frames
8. Color frames

Every product is tagged **Men**, **Women**, and **Unisex** as requested. Customer-facing discovery should emphasize shape, color, and style because identical gender collections would provide little additional value.

## Product-page SEO

Each product should include:

- A unique editorial name plus a searchable shape term
- Unique description based on visible color, shape, lens, and invoice specifications
- Model code retained as SKU and MPN
- Three descriptive image alt texts
- Product, Offer, Breadcrumb, and Organization structured data
- Price, availability, shipping, and finalized returns information
- Internal links to its shape, color, and polarized collections

Do not claim UV protection, scratch resistance, warranty coverage, or a return period until those facts are confirmed.

## Content strategy

Priority guides:

1. How to choose sunglasses for your face shape
2. Polarized vs. non-polarized sunglasses
3. Best sunglasses for travel and bright holidays
4. How to style statement sunglasses
5. Rectangle, cat-eye, round, and geometric frame guides
6. Sunglasses care and storage

Each guide should link to relevant collections and products. Original customer photos and reviews will be more valuable than publishing large volumes of generic AI-written articles.
